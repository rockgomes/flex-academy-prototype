FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still not sure? Bring your question to the free webinar.

How much time do I need each week?

Launch is self-paced, so you fit it around your job. Plan time for the live group sessions, and for the real work on your first unit: viewings, calls and paperwork. Scale has one group session a week for twelve weeks, and a monthly 1:1 with a founder.

How much money do I need to start?

It depends on your city and your model: renting a flat to sublet with permission, or owning one. That is why the first module is about the numbers for one unit, before you sign anything. We do not promise an income.

Which countries does this work in?

Flex Academy is taught in English and is open to all English-speaking countries, such as the UK, the US, Canada and Australia. The method is the same everywhere. The local rules are not, so we show you how to check yours.

What about local rules and licences?

Short-term rental rules change from city to city: permits, limits on nights, taxes and landlord consent. The programmes show you how to check them before you commit. They are not legal advice.

Is there a refund?

Prices and terms are not set yet. When they are, the refund policy will be on this page, in plain words, before you pay anything.

What happens on the strategy call?

20 to 30 minutes with the team. They look at your units, your target and your city, and tell you if Scale fits. If it does not, they say so and point you to the right first step.
